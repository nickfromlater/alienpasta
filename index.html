Alien Pasta Lab

Cook silly space food with Blorp

Support

Something not working, a question, or an idea? Email hello@wobbet.app and say which device you play on. A person reads every message.

How do I play?

Pick a dish and follow Blorp. Each step shows what to do (tap, hold, swirl, roll, swipe and more), and a little hand shows you how. There is no way to fail: every dish ends with Blorp gobbling it up.

What is free?

Four recipes (Alien Pasta, Moon Pizza, Comet Cupcakes and Nebula Smoothie) in Blorp's Starship. One purchase, The Whole Galaxy, opens every other recipe and kitchen, the Alien Friends, and every recipe added later. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing else to buy.

What are star noodles?

The coins in Klink's Space Shop. Children earn them by cooking (and a free spin of the Lucky Wheel each day) and spend them on hats, outfits and treats for Blorp. Star noodles can't be bought with money.

Why does it ask a math question?

That is the grown-ups' gate. Buying, restoring and links out of the game sit behind it, so children can't reach them by accident.

I bought The Whole Galaxy. How do I get it back?

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the pause menu, tap For grown-ups, then Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

The purchase says it is waiting for approval

That is Ask to Buy. Everything opens by itself as soon as the request is approved.

Where is progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes the stickers, outfits, star noodles and cooked dishes. Your purchase is never lost: it comes back with Restore purchase.

Does it need the internet?

No. Alien Pasta Lab plays offline. Only buying or restoring The Whole Galaxy talks to the App Store.

Are the real recipes safe for kids to make?

Every dish has a real-world recipe in Blorp's cookbook to make together. They are written for a child and a grown-up: the grown-up does anything with knives, the stove or the oven. Check ingredients for allergies first.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases at reportaproblem.apple.com.
